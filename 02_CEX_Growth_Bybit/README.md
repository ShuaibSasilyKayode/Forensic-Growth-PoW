# CASE STUDY: Driving $15M+ Monthly Trading Volume Increase
**Project:** Bybit (Global Crypto Exchange)
**Role:** Regional Associate & Client Engagement Lead

## 🛠 The Challenge
In a highly competitive CEX market, the goal was to capture market share in a specific region and increase the active trader base for both Spot and Futures products without overextending the marketing budget.

## 🚀 My Strategy
* **Data-Driven Lead Gen:** Utilized **Dune Analytics and Nansen** to identify trending on-chain assets and "Whale" wallets, allowing for targeted outreach to high-volume traders.
* **Affiliate & KOL Network Engineering:** Scaled a network of **50+ high-impact community partnerships** and influencers, aligning their incentives with platform volume growth.
* **Forensic Liquidity Monitoring:** Performed daily risk assessments on P2P corridors to ensure zero-slippage for large trades.

## 📊 The Results
* **$15M+ Volume Growth:** Successfully drove an increase in monthly trading volume across Spot and Futures markets.
* **210% User Acquisition:** Engineered a scalable pipeline that led to a massive increase in international user sign-ups.
* **35% Reduction in CAC:** Optimized marketing spend by aligning with local KOLs using data-driven auditing.
