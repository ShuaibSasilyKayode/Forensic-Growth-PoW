# CASE STUDY: Strategic BD & Ecosystem Expansion
**Project:** Cenoa (Fintech/Web3 Savings Super-App)
**Role:** Strategic Growth & BD Lead

## 🛠 The Challenge
Cenoa required a rapid Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy to dominate emerging markets. The objective was to build a "trust layer" for non-crypto natives by securing high-impact partnerships and optimizing capital allocation.

## 🚀 Business Development Execution
* **Partnership Pipeline Engineering:** Identified and secured **50+ high-impact partnerships** with ecosystem players (wallets, on-ramps, and regional fintech hubs).
* **On-Chain Revenue Intelligence:** Leveraged **Dune Analytics and Nansen** to map liquidity flows and target high-potential crypto projects for integration.
* **Capital Allocation:** Directly managed **$50k+ monthly budgets**, optimizing spend across partner networks to maximize ROI and lower acquisition costs.
* **Relationship Management:** Built long-term alliances with international stakeholders, achieving a **210% increase in regional user growth** within 12 months.

## 📊 Business Impact
* **210% User Surge:** Scaled the regional footprint through a partner-led growth model.
* **35% Reduction in CAC:** Optimized lead generation through data-driven auditing and strategic KOL alignment.
* **30% Friction Reduction:** Identified systemic hurdles in the KYC flow, improving the "Lead-to-Active" conversion rate.

## 🛠 Methodology: The BD Tiering Matrix
*I utilized the following framework to vet and prioritize all 50+ partnerships:*

| Tier | Partner Type | Strategic Goal | Selection Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | Tier-1 Exchanges / Wallets | Mass Liquidity | >100k DAU, On-chain verified volume |
| **Tier 2** | Regional Fintech Influencers | Trust & Education | High engagement rate, Local authority |
| **Tier 3** | Niche DeFi Communities | Yield Alpha Seekers | Active Discord/Telegram, High TVL/user |
