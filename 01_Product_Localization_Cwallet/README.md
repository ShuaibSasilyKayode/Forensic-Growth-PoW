# CASE STUDY: Reducing Onboarding Friction through Technical Localization
**Project:** Cwallet (DeFi Infrastructure)  
**Objective:** Scale international user acquisition and lower technical barriers to entry.

## 🛠 The Challenge
Cwallet faced high drop-off rates in emerging markets due to complex DeFi terminology and non-localized UI. Technical documentation was too dense for retail users, leading to a high Cost Per Acquisition (CPA).

## 🚀 My Strategy
* **Automated Localization Pipeline:** Managed the end-to-end workflow using Crowdin/Lokalise to ensure consistency across 15+ languages.
* **Transcreation vs. Translation:** Adapted complex Web3 concepts (staking, self-custody) into culturally resonant "Meme" culture and local financial analogies.
* **Friction Audit:** Identified UI jargon that caused onboarding failure and collaborated with the product team to simplify the flow.

## 📊 The Results
* **30% Reduction in Onboarding Friction:** Significantly increased the "Install-to-Active" conversion rate.
* **35% Reduction in CAC:** Optimized marketing spend by aligning with local KOLs using the new localized messaging.
* **Scale:** Onboarded 500+ active users through strategic influencer alignment.

## 🧰 Tech Stack
* **Localization:** Crowdin/Lokalise
* **Analytics:** Dune Analytics, Nansen
* **Growth:** Telegram, Discord, X (Twitter)
